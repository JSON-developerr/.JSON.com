About Me

Hey! I'm Jason. I make games, write code, mess around with music, draw sometimes, and generally have way more ideas than I have time to actually make.

Game development is probably the thing I spend the most time doing. I like taking an idea that exists entirely in my head and slowly turning it into something I can actually walk around in, play, break, fix, and eventually let other people experience. I also just really like programming, even when I spend an hour fixing something that turned out to be one stupid line of code.

I'm not trying to make this website super professional or turn myself into some kind of brand. I'm just a person who likes making stuff, and this is where I put that stuff.

If you've somehow found your way here, hi! :P

What I Make

Most of what I make is games. I especially like making games with exploration, secrets, weird mechanics, stories, and things that reward you for wondering, "Wait, can I actually do that?"

Basically, if it involves games, programming, music, drawing, writing, or doing something unnecessarily complicated because I thought it would be cool, there's a decent chance I'll try making it.

This Website

I made this website because I don't really like social media.

I still use some social platforms to show people my games, but I don't want an algorithmic feed to be the main home for everything I make. I wanted my own little place where I could post development updates, screenshots, games, music, writing, random thoughts, and whatever else I feel like sharing.

There's no algorithm deciding what you should see here. If I make something and want it on the website, I put it on the website. Old posts can stay here instead of disappearing underneath hundreds of newer ones.

It's basically my own little corner of the internet. Look around, read something, play one of my games, and maybe poke at suspicious things that look clickable.

Why I Make Games

Mostly?

Because I fucking love making games.

I love being able to think of something completely stupid like "What if the player could secretly steal this movement suit and completely destroy the rest of the game's level design?" and then realize that, because I'm the developer, I can just do that.

Games combine basically everything I'm interested in. Programming makes everything function, art gives it an identity, music gives it a feeling, writing gives everything a reason to exist, and level design turns all of that into something another person can actually experience.

It's also incredibly satisfying watching a project slowly become real. A room starts as some boxes, then it gets lighting, mechanics, sounds, details, secrets, and suddenly it's an actual place inside an actual game.

I don't know exactly where game development will take me. I just know I want to keep making things.

Boundaries

I'm happy to talk about my games, programming, art, music, and other things I share here. If you find a bug, have feedback, ect, that's cool!

That doesn't mean everything about my life is public, though. Please don't try to dig up personal information about me, figure out where I live or go to school, track down people I know, or otherwise turn normal curiosity into investigating my private life.

I've made mistakes in the past, and I've learned a lot from them. I'm focused on improving, creating, and moving forward.

Please keep comments focused on my games and creations. Personal matters, old events, or things unrelated to my projects are private, and I appreciate people respecting that boundary.

Basically: be normal.
