End of Year Roadmap

10/6/2026

At the time of writing this, there's 2 months left in 2026.

And I want to get my current projects finished and released before the end of the year.

With that said...

What Are the Next 2 Months Looking Like?

Well.

A lot.

Luckily, I'm actually in a pretty good position right now.

HTK is basically done.

There's still some stuff to finish up, but the actual game is pretty much there. After spending so much time working on it, it's weird seeing it sitting this close to being finished.

And then there's Floodgate.

Which is...

in the endgame.

I'm currently around door 95, with the main game going all the way to door 100.

That means the giant pile of rooms I've been making for what feels like forever is finally starting to run out.

I'm not saying Floodgate itself is completely finished after that. There are still things I want to add and improve, and there are DLC plans..

But the main game is almost done. All features are made, all mechanics are made, Its Just like 3 cutscenes and 5 rooms.

So the next month Is mostly going to be about getting these projects across the finish line.

I'm not going to pretend I can perfectly predict what every single week will look like.

Some things will take longer than expected.

Some things will take Shorter than expected. (Hopefully)

But the goal is pretty simple:

Finish the projects.

Get them into a state where I can actually release them instead of constantly saying "yeah, it'll be done eventually."

And hopefully, by the time 2026 ends, I'll be able to look back and say:

I actually finished all of this.

That's the plan, anyway.

There's still a lot of work between here and November.

But for the first time, most of the things—wait what?

November?????

"What happened to the 2 months?"

uh... uhh.....

*cof cof*

DLC

*cof cof.*

...

Yeah.

Apparently I forgot that I had plans for the rest of the year.

So here's what the end of the year is looking like.

November

November is going to be a pretty big month.

The main goal is to get the major projects into a place where I can actually start showing them off properly.

Floodgate is getting closer and closer to the end of its main development, and I've also got HTK and other stuff that I want to show.

So November is basically going to be:

"LOOK AT ALL THE THINGS I MADE"

Which sounds pretty cool.

I've been working on these projects for a long time, so being able to actually put everything together and show it off is something I'm really looking forward to.

And then

December

December is apparently just DLC month.

I don't know how this happened.

I started looking at the calendar and realized that instead of calmly finishing everything and taking a break, I had somehow scheduled myself to spend December making expansions.

It's basically going to be:

"Okay, the main game is done."

"Cool."

"Now let's make more game."

Which honestly sounds pretty fun.

I also want to make a little Christmas gift for everyone.

Nothing massive.

Just something small and fun to end the year with.

Because if I'm going to spend December making DLC anyway, I might as well throw something nice in there too.

And Then...

That's basically it.

After the DLC stuff, the Christmas gift, and everything else I've planned for the end of the year...

The games can die.

Not literally.

Please don't bury my games in the backyard.

I mean I'm going to stop developing them.

I've been working on games for a LONG time.

And as much as I love doing it, I think I'm going to need a proper break after all of this.

I just want to stop for a bit.

Do other things.

Exist without having a Unity project sitting in the back of my head going:

**hey**

**hey**

**remember me**

**you should probably finish that room**

No.

I won't.

I'm going on break.

But that's still a little ways away.

For now, there's a lot left to do.

Floodgate still has its final stretch.

November is coming.

December is apparently DLC.

There's a Christmas gift somewhere in there.

And then...

I think that's it.

For the first time in a while, I can actually see the point where I get to put all of this down for a little while.

And honestly?

That sounds pretty damn nice.

And, uh...
