Moving Forward..

ok so now that the games are done, what now?

well, i'm going on break for awhile.

sure, i'll still be slowly working on Cold Front, but unlike the last few months, things are going to be slowing down a TON. i don't want to immediately jump from finishing two massive projects into another few months of nonstop work.

i've spent a lot of time working on HTK and Floodgate, and i'm really happy that i finally got them to a point where i can show them off. but honestly, i think i need some time to just pause, instead of immediately worrying about what's next.

that doesn't mean i'm quitting game development or anything like that. Cold Front is still happening, and i've got plenty of ideas for the future. i just don't want to rush any of them.

so for the next little while, expect fewer updates, fewer blog posts, and probably a lot less happening around here. i'll still share stuff when i have something worth showing, but i'm not going to force myself to make progress just for the sake of having something to post.

i've spent months getting everything ready, and now i finally get to take a breath.

thanks to everyone who's followed my projects so far. it genuinely means a lot.

see ya around
