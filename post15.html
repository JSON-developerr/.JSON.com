PRESENTAT.SON ANNOUNCEMENTS!!

Okay, so uh...

HTK AND FLOODGATE are basicly finished.

WHAT.

Both games are done! Well, mostly. There's still some final polish left to do, fixing little things, making sure everything works properly, and all that fun stuff.

But the actual games? The main work? DONE.

Floodgate is finally finished after all this time, and HTK is right there with it. It's genuinely weird being able to say that after spending so long working on both of them.

I think I might actually be allowed to take a break soon. Maybe. We'll see.

PRESENTAT.SON?

Presentat.son is basically my own version of a Nintendo Direct.

You know how Nintendo gets everyone together to show off upcoming games, announce new stuff, show trailers, and generally make people lose their minds over things they weren't expecting?

Yeah. It's that, but for my projects.

I'll be using Presentat.son to show off what I've been working on, announce upcoming projects and updates, share trailers, and give you a look at what's coming next.

Basically, instead of making a bunch of separate announcements whenever something happens, I can put everything together into one presentation.

Which is convenient, because apparently I have a LOT of stuff to talk about.

And yes, there are things coming that I haven't shown yet.

I'm not saying anything else.
