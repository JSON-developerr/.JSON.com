Hollow Knight: Together Is Alive!!!

9/17/26

Heya!

So uh...

It's been a while lol.

If you've been following what I've been making recently, you probably know exactly what happened.

**Floodgate happened.**

I started working on Floodgate a LOT more, got completely carried away with it, and HKT kinda just sat there for a while.

BUT!

I'm working on Hollow Knight: Together again!

And instead of immediately adding 700 new areas and making the game take another 4 years to finish, I've mostly been going back through what already exists and making it better.

So here's some of the stuff I've been doing :P

OLD AREAS ARE GETTING REDONE!!

One of the biggest things I've noticed coming back to HKT is just how much my game development has changed since I made some of these areas.

Some of them are OLD.

And you can tell lol.

So I've been going back through older areas and reworking their visuals without completely throwing away what was already there.

Plasmium Plateau got a pretty big visual rework, and other areas have gotten changes too.

-->

I've been redoing backgrounds, changing some of the colors, adding more detail, fixing things that looked weird, and generally making the older parts of the game fit better with the newer ones.

I'm trying not to completely remake every area from scratch.

If something already works, it can stay.

It just might get a new coat of paint.
