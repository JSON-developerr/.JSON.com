Hollow Knight: Together Is Alive!!!

9/17/26

Heya!

So uh...

It's been a while lol.

If you've been following what I've been making recently, you probably know exactly what happened.

**Floodgate happened.**

I started working on Floodgate a LOT more, got completely carried away with it, and HKT kinda just sat there for a while.

BUT!

I'm working on Hollow Knight: Together again!

And instead of immediately adding 700 new areas and making the game take another 4 years to finish, I've mostly been going back through what already exists and making it better.

So here's some of the stuff I've been doing :P

OLD AREAS ARE GETTING REDONE!!

One of the biggest things I've noticed coming back to HKT is just how much my game development has changed since I made some of these areas.

Some of them are OLD.

And you can tell lol.

So I've been going back through older areas and reworking their visuals without completely throwing away what was already there.

Some of the older areas were VERY static.

You had the player moving around and...

that's about it.

So now a lot more of the environment actually moves!

Plasmium Plateau got a pretty big visual rework, and other areas have gotten changes too.

--> Small background animations, environmental stuff, effects, and other little details have been added to make areas feel less like a bunch of platforms sitting in front of a picture.

It's a pretty small thing individually, but all of it together makes the game feel WAY nicer.

Also I added a frontflip when you pogo something.

THE PALE CHILD GOT BUFFED

Remember this little guy?

Well.

He's stronger now.

The Pure Child fight has been buffed and changed around to make it more interesting and actually feel like one of the bigger fights near the end of Act 1.

He's faster, more dangerous, and generally less willing to let you beat the shit out of him for free.

Here's a clip of the new fight:

-->

I still have some balancing to do, but I'm MUCH happier with how the fight feels now.

Tiny vessel has learned more violence.

OTHER TINY THINGS!!!

There's also been a bunch of smaller stuff that doesn't really deserve an entire section by itself.

The Pale Garden got nerfed because apparently I decided it needed to hate the player.

There's a new tutorial area.

Singleplayer has gotten more work.

Some UI stuff has been changed.

Movement and little animations have gotten touched up.

And there are a bunch of tiny fixes and changes scattered around the game that I have already forgotten about lol.

Basically:

**HKT is being worked on again.**

What now?

I'm still working on Floodgate.

HKT coming back does NOT mean I abandoned Floodgate and ran into another project again lol.

I'm working on both.

Sometimes I'll feel like working on Floodgate.

Sometimes I'll feel like working on HKT.

And right now, I've been having a lot of fun going back through HKT and seeing just how much I can improve now compared to when I originally made some of this stuff.

There's still a lot left to do.

But I'm working on it again.

And it feels really nice to be back :)

More eventually!

See ya.
